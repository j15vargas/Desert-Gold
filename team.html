Meet Our Leadership Team

Chip Simmons

Co-Founder

Chip is a Co-Founder of Desert Gold and is responsible for investor sourcing, deal origination, investor relationship management, executing acquisition strategies and financial matters.
Chip is also a Co-Founder of Tokenized Energy , a new platform which tokenizes interests in upstream oil & gas assets on the Base blockchain.
He also serves as CEO of Titanium Exploration Partners, LLC, with responsibility for managing its remaining Mid-Continent assets. Titanium deployed approx. $600mm+ for two private equity funds (each with $15B+ of AUM) in the Eagle Ford Shale play in South Texas, the Scoop/Stack plays in the Mid-Continent and the Delaware Basin over a 10 year period.
Prior to Titanium, Chip served as Managing Director of Clear Creek Capital, a Dallas, TX-based oil and gas investment firm, which originated and managed investments in AB Resources (Appalachian Basin), Kerns Petroleum (Permian Basin/Eagle Ford Shale/Granite Wash), AleAnna Resources (Italy) and Torch Resources, for a New York City-based private equity fund. He served on the Boards of AB Resources and Kerns Petroleum.
Chip graduated summa cum laude from Yale University with a double major in Economics & Political Science, then received a JD from the University of Virginia.

Adrian Macias

Co-Founder

Adrian is responsible for Desert Gold's business development, relationship management with our key industry partners, and investment decision-making.
Adrian is also a Co-Founder in Tokenized Energy which provides a transparent marketplace for investors to acquire mineral rights and non-operated working interests on the blockchain.
Adrian is an active investor in upstream oil and gas assets (primarily non-op) and owns assets in the Permian, Powder River Basin, Scoop and Bakken shale basins.
Prior to co-founding Tokenized Energy and Desert Gold Oil & Gas, Adrian served as the Executive Vice President of Land & Business Development for Titanium Exploration Partners, LLC which managed minerals and non-operated working interests in the Permian, Scoop/Stack, and Eagle Ford shale plays. Adrian oversaw the divestment of tens of millions of dollars of assets on behalf of two global private equity investors.
Adrian started his career in oil and gas as a partner at Purple Land Management (a Fort Worth, TX based land and technology consulting company). Adrian, as Vice President of Corporate Development, was integral in scaling the business to over $50mm a year in revenue and the development of a patented, GIS-based oil and gas asset management platform (Overdrive). Adrian led the sale of PLM to a Dallas, TX, based private equity firm in 2018 and joined Titanium Exploration partners shortly thereafter.
Adrian is a graduate of the Texas Tech University School of Law and holds a B.A. degree in Government from the University of Texas at Austin.

Aaron McCamant

Co-Founder

Aaron is responsible for sourcing new investment opportunities and for managing all of Desert Gold's land related matters, including evaluating and negotiating all land-related documents (including JOAs, oil and gas leases, etc.), coordinated title reviews with outside land firms of our acquisition targets, conducting our closings, performing asset management services post-acquisition and coordinating our exits, including assembling data rooms and managing our outside relationships with investment bankers and investors. In addition to daily land/asset management items, he focuses on identifying opportunities to extract more value from the asset base through acreage trades and other opportunistic development opportunities.
Prior to founding Desert Gold Oil & Gas, LLC, Aaron served as Vice President of Land at Titanium Exploration Partners (“TEP”), where he performed the services described above.
Prior to joining TEP, he was the Director of Client Acquisitions at Purple Land Management in Fort Worth, TX where he managed due diligence projects for approximately $20 billion of A&D and financing transactions across the Midland Basin, Delaware Basin, Eagle Ford, Oklahoma, and Rockies.
Aaron is a graduate of Texas Christian University and holds a Bachelor of Science in Economics.
