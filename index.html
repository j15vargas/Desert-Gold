Trusted Leaders in Oil & Gas Acquisitions

At Desert Gold Oil & & Gas, LLC, we specialize in the fair and strategic acquisition of mineral rights, royalties, and non-operated working interests across the United States. Led by co-Founders Chip Simmons, Adrian Macias and Aaron McCamant, we are fair and honest with a long track record of working with multiple investors to acquire and divest high-quality upstream oil and gas assets. The team has worked together in different capacities for over a decade

Strategy

  • Nationwide Approach: Desert Gold is active in all major U.S. basins, with recent tranactions in the Permian (both Midland and Delaware), Bakken, Utica, and Marcellus shale plays.
  • Easy To Work With: Desert Gold acquires mineral and non-operated positions through marketed and unsolicited processes and have closed transactions with hundreds of counterparties from individual mineral owners to large, public oil and gas companies.
  • Ground Game Experts: Desert Gold also develops strategic leasing programs - ground games- to acquire large positions in targeted portions of shale plays.
